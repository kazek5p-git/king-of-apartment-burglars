Review Notes

The app does not require login, demo credentials, payments, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or external hardware. It is a fictional audio game with abstract lock timing mechanics, native iOS menus, VoiceOver support, a sound tutorial, and a training mode.

To test the core gameplay, open the game, start training or a new run, listen for the lock cue, react with Enter at the correct moment, switch to the handle mode when the lock opens, and enter before the short window ends.
